y.append(3)


$ the shared list becomes:
[1, 2, 3]

$ KEY_IDEA:

same object.

Change the object → everyone
referencing it sees the change.

$ CORRECT_OUTPUT:

60% got it right. 📈

> WHY?

Many people calculate:
(2 ** 3) ** 2 = 8 ** 2 = 64


But that's NOT how Python
evaluates chained exponents.

Exponentiation is evaluated
from right to left:
2 ** (3 ** 2)


$ first:
3 ** 2 → 9


$ then:
2 ** 9 → 512


$ KEY_IDEA:

** has right-to-left
associativity.
